Viet Bake Shop Simulator

the main character

You inherit Bà's bakery on Saigon Street; it looks cozy, with pixel bánh mì and cà phê sữa đá, but underneath it's an economics engine that doesn't let you off easy.

Charge too much and customers walk out—each type of customer reacts to price differently—while eight rival bakeries undercut you and the economy swings from boom to recession to inflation, whether you're ready or not. Every day ends with a real income statement, balance sheet and cash-flow statement that always balance; take out a loan, and you'll learn what leverage means the hard way.
